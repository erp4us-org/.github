# Discussions

Use [the shared forum](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/discussions) for questions, scenarios and proposals. Search first, give a fictional example, distinguish present behavior from goals and critique ideas respectfully. English is our primary language; other languages are welcome. No technical solution is required. Do not post confidential or customer information, and clearly identify substantive AI-generated contributions. Follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [report vulnerabilities privately](SECURITY.md).

Discussions are not approved requirements. Follow the [community requirements workflow](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/blob/main/docs/requirements/community-workflow.md): maintainers review proposed catalog changes before implementation work is tracked in linked issues. The version-controlled catalog is authoritative for approved requirements.
