# Code of Conduct

Help create a welcoming environment for people of all backgrounds, identities and experience levels. Be respectful, assume good faith when reasonable, and provide constructive feedback on ideas and work. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and abusive retaliation are not acceptable.

This applies in project spaces and when representing the project. Contact the repository owner privately through a contact method listed on their GitHub profile; if none is available or the owner is involved, use GitHub's abuse reporting. Do not file confidential reports in a public issue. Maintainers handle reports discreetly and may remove content or restrict participation proportionately. Appeals may go to an uninvolved maintainer where available.
